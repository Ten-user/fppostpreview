MOBU OA HAO OA SENYEHA. LIJALO TSA HAO LI A FOKOLA. NA UA TSEBA HOBANENG?

U se u le lilemo u lema — empa selemo le selemo, kotulo ea fokola.
Mobu oa thatafala. Lijalo ha li sa hola hantle joalo ka pele.

'Nete ke ena:
manyolo a lik'hemik'hale a fana ka tlhahiso ea nako e khutšoane — empa a senya mobu oa hao ha nako e ntse e ea.

Mobu o lahleheloa ke matla.
Dibaktheria tse molemo lia fela.
Qetellong — o sala o lema mobu o shoeleng.

👉 Ke ka lebaka leo re hlahisitseng:
Kabo Organic Fertilizer

✔ 100% ea tlhaho
✔ E hlahlojoa laboratori
✔ E bolokehile bakeng sa mobu, lijalo le tikoloho

Se ka hare ho eona (Guaranteed Analysis):

🌿 Lintho tsa tlhaho: 18–20%

🌿 Nitrogen (N): 0.4%

🌿 Phosphorus (P): 28–30 ppm

🌿 Potassium (K): 4200–4500 ppm

🌿 Calcium (Ca): 1700–2300 ppm

🌿 Magnesium (Mg): 1700–2200 ppm

🌿 pH: 8.9–9.0

E thusa joang mobu oa hao?

✅ E matlafatsa mobu hore o fephe lijalo hantle

✅ E khutlisa dibaktheria tse molemo

✅ E ntlafatsa sebopeho sa mobu (metsi a lula hantle, ha o ome kapele)

✅ E lokisa pH hore limatlafatsi li fumanehe

✅ E sireletsa tikoloho — ha e silafatse metsi

U ka e sebelisa hokae?

✔ Meroho — 200–400g ka mokoting

✔ Poone — 1–2 matsoho ka mokoting

✔ Lifate tsa litholoana — 2–5kg ho potoloha sefate

✔ Lipeo — kopanya 1 karolo ea manyolo le 3 tsa mobu

✔ Masimo — 1–2 tone ka hekitara

Hobaneng u lokela ho qala hona joale?

Manyolo a lik'hemik'hale a fana ka liphetho kapele — empa a senya bokamoso ba mobu oa hao.

Manyolo a tlhaho a haha mobu butle — empa a u fa kotulo e tsitsitseng selemo le selemo.

Mobu o betere =
✔ Lijalo tse matla
✔ Kotulo e ngata
✔ Tšireletso nakong ea komello

Se emele ho fihlela mobu oa hao o se o fokotsehile haholo.
