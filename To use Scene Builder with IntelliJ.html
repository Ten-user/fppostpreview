SOIL ISN'T DIRT. IT'S ALIVE. AND YOUR CROPS ARE HUNGRY.

Let's talk about something most farmers overlook — soil health.

Most of us plant, apply chemical fertilizers, harvest, and repeat season after season. Meanwhile the soil is dying. Structure is breaking down. Microbes are gone. Yields are dropping. The land that fed your parents is barely feeding you.

That's exactly why we made Kabo Organic Fertilizer.

What is it?

It's a 100% organic, granular fertilizer — not a chemical cocktail. It's made from decomposed organic matter that's been properly composted and laboratory tested. Every single batch.

What's actually in it?

This isn't guesswork. Here's the guaranteed analysis:

🌿 Organic Matter: 18–20%

🌿 Total Nitrogen (N): 0.4%

🌿 Available Phosphorus (P): 28–30 ppm

🌿 Potassium (K): 4200–4500 ppm

🌿 Calcium (Ca): 1700–2300 ppm

🌿 Magnesium (Mg): 1700–2200 ppm

🌿 pH: 8.9–9.0

🌿 Heavy Metals: Within safe agricultural limits

What does it DO for your soil?

✅ Improves soil fertility — feeds the soil so the soil can feed your crops

✅ Enhances microbial activity — brings back the bacteria and organisms that make soil WORK

✅ Improves soil structure — better water retention, better drainage, less crusting

✅ Helps balance soil pH — so nutrients actually become available to your plants

✅ Safe and environmentally friendly — no chemical runoff into your water, no poisoned soil for the next generation

What crops can you use it on?

Everything.

🥬 Vegetables — 200–400g per planting hole

🌽 Maize — 1–2 handfuls per planting hole

🍎 Fruit Trees — 2–5kg around the tree, twice a year

🌱 Seedlings — mix 1 part fertilizer with 3 parts soil

🌾 Field Crops — 1–2 tons per hectare

Best used as:

✔ Basal dressing

✔ Soil conditioner

✔ All-purpose organic fertilizer

Why this matters right now:

Chemical fertilizers give you a quick boost then destroy your soil over time. Organic fertilizer works slower but builds your soil UP. Year after year, your land gets better. Your yields get more consistent. Your soil holds more water during drought. Your crops resist pests better because the soil is actually healthy.

One 50kg bag covers a significant area depending on your crop and application rate. And because it's slow-release, it keeps feeding your soil for weeks — not days like chemicals.

This isn't new science. This is how farming worked for generations before chemicals took over. We just made it easier to use.
